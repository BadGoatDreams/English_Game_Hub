📖 The Hustle Vocab

  • Gig Economy: Temporary, flexible jobs.
  • Blue-Collar: Manual labor or trade jobs.
  • White-Collar: Office or desk jobs.
  • Transferable Skills: Abilities used in any job.
  • Enlistment Bonus: Money paid for joining the military.
  • Overtime: Working more than your normal hours.
  • Graveyard Shift: Working through the night.
  • Leverage: Using what you have to get a deal.

🚨 Grammar Patrol

  • "I have 2 years experience" ➡️ "I have 2 years **of** experience."
  • "I am finding a job" ➡️ "I am **looking for** a job."
  • "I work as barista" ➡️ "I work as **a** barista."
  • "I'm graduate in..." ➡️ "I **graduated** with a degree in..."
