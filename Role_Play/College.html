📚 Vocabulary: The Basics

  • Tuition 💰: The money you pay to study.
  • Quota 📊: The maximum number of students allowed.
  • Financial Aid / Bonus 🤝: Free money from the school/job.
  • Vibe Check 😎: Judging someone by personality, not grades.
  • Recruiter 🧑‍💼: The person who decides your future.

🏛️ Types of Schools

  • The Elites (Ivy League) 🦄
    Requires perfect grades (4.0). Very expensive.
  • State College 🏫
    Average requirements. Good for normal students.
  • Party School 🎉
    Grades don't matter. Just have money and be fun.
  • The Workplace 🍔
    Flight Attendant, Military, or Burgers. No grades needed.

🎯 Student Goals

  • The CEO 💼: Wants Business School. Wants to be rich.
  • The Artist 🎨: Wants Liberal Arts. Wants to be unique.
  • The Survivor 🧟: Just wants a Visa. Will go anywhere.
  • The Athlete ⚽: Wants Sports Academy. GPA is low, but plays ball.

🧑‍💼 Instructions: College Recruiters

(Ivy League, State, Business, Arts)

  • 1. Check the Stats: Does the student meet your requirements (GPA/SAT)? If not, reject them.
  • 2. Check the Money: Can they pay full price? 🤑
  • 3. Use your Aid: You have a limited budget for scholarships. Give it only to the best students.
  • 4. Watch the Quota: You only have a few spots (e.g., 2 or 4). Don't fill them too fast!

Strategy: Be picky. Make them beg.

🛠️ Instructions: Job Recruiters

(Flight Attendant, Military & Burger Shop)

  • 1. Ignore Grades: You don't care about SAT or GPA. 🚮
  • 2. Check the Body/Vibe: Are they healthy? Friendly? Alive?
  • 3. The Pitch: Tell them: "College is a waste of money. Come get a salary with us!"
  • 4. Steal Students: Look for students who were rejected by colleges. They are your targets.

Strategy: Find the desperate students.

🗣️ Student Dialogue

Goal: Convince the Recruiter

  • "My GPA is 4.0 and I love studying." 🤓
  • "My grades are bad, but my budget is unlimited." 🤑
  • "Please! I have nowhere else to go!" 😭
  • "I see your tuition is high. Do you have Financial Aid?" 💸

🧐 Recruiter Dialogue

Goal: Fill spots, save money.

  • "Your SAT score is tragic. Next!" 📉
  • "We can accept you, but you must pay Full Tuition." 🏦
  • "I can offer a $20k scholarship. Take it or leave it." 🤝
  • "Sorry, we have reached our Quota." 🚫

🚨 THE GRAMMAR POLICE 🚨

  • Goal: Catch bad grammar.
  • Punishment: Force them to read a Poem/Tongue Twister.
  • "Freeze! ✋ Did you just say 'I go'?"
  • "That is a violation. Code 404: Bad English."
  • "Read this poem LOUDLY or go to the back of the line!"

⛔ Common Traps

  • "I am agree." ➡️ "I agree."
  • "It depends of..." ➡️ "It depends ON..."
  • "I have 20 years old." ➡️ "I AM 20 years old."
  • "He work here." ➡️ "He workS here."

If you make these mistakes, the Police will catch you!
